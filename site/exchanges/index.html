Exchanges

Compatibility is a starting point. Check the whole setup.

Cornix connects to many exchanges. Three things still have to line up before the NoGuru bot can trade in your account: Cornix has to serve you, the exchange has to accept you, and the account has to work for this setup.

Check Cornix service eligibility first.

Cornix’s terms exclude several countries, among them the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada. The rule covers more than where you live; citizenship can count too. Read the list before you sign up for either service.

Three checks

Can it connect? The exchange, the exact website your account is on and the product type must be supported by Cornix. Spot and futures support are different things.

Can the bot run there? The exchange needs the coins the bot trades, spot trading through an API key, and a sub-account for the bot’s money.

May you use it? Your country, the exchange’s legal entity for your account and local rules must all allow it.

The exchanges Cornix lists

NoGuru’s most-tested setup is Binance. For any other exchange, a Cornix connection alone does not mean the NoGuru bot has been run there.

The website your account is on can change the answer.

An exchange’s regional sites are different companies with different rules. Cornix excludes Binance.us and Bybit.eu outright, and its pages disagree about OKX’s European site. A similar name or logo does not make two accounts interchangeable.

The NoGuru offer excludes UK residents.

Before connecting a real account

Do not use a VPN, a false country or any other workaround to reach an exchange that does not accept you.

  • Confirm your country of residence and the exchange’s legal entity for your account.
  • Check spot trading through an API key, the pairs you need and the minimum order sizes.
  • Use a sub-account that holds only the money for the bot.
  • Allow trading on the key and keep withdrawals switched off.

A setup guide is not an endorsement.

The links above explain how Cornix connects to each exchange. They say nothing about an exchange’s safety or solvency, and they do not tell you how much to keep there. Follow the exchange’s current instructions, and never share the secret part of a key.
