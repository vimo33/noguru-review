Once connected and configured, Cornix automatically places NoGuru’s planned buy and target-sale orders in your exchange account. Buys follow price levels rather than a fixed weekly or monthly calendar. A target sale still depends on the price reaching it and the order executing.

01 / BUY IN STEPS

02 / AIM FOR A RECOVERY

A repeatable process

Why we built this bot.

Buying crypto is only the beginning. There are more decisions to make: when to add, when to aim for a sale, and how to follow the same plan when prices move. We built NoGuru to automate that routine through a defined set of rules. Cornix places the orders in your exchange account, so you do not have to enter each trade yourself.

You choose how much to commit, check the setup and keep an eye on your holdings. Your funds stay in your own exchange account.

Understand what can go wrong.

The strategy has no stop-loss. A lower average purchase price is not protection against a lasting loss.

Read the full risk guide →
  • !

    A coin may never recover.

    Holdings can remain below cost for months, fall much further, or lose their value.

    There is no promised holding period. Money in an open position is still exposed to the market.

  • !

How is the approximately +34.0% calculated?

The original source page calculates the cumulative completed result from its historical gross monthly summary and window length: 11.24 × 13.14 ÷ 4.345 ≈ 33.99%, rounded here to about +34.0%. The inputs are already rounded.

This is a summary on the model’s allocated funds for 1 August–1 November 2026, before trading fees. It excludes losses on open holdings and does not deduct all subscription and trading costs. The historical monthly input is not an expected future monthly return.

The public rows do not provide each cycle’s monetary cost or allocation weight. Their individual percentages cannot be summed or compounded into a daily profit curve.

Why is there no total portfolio return here?

The source does not establish a common, fully documented allocation basis for completed profit, open holdings and reserve cash. It also does not supply the synchronized values and full costs needed to reconcile portfolio equity.

A separate published “funds in trades” measure is 115.1%. Its cost-versus-market valuation, allocation denominator and reinvestment treatment are not fully documented. It is not shown as a cash-allocation gauge or used to calculate net return.

Subtracting 12.2 from 34.0 would combine different bases and produce an unsupported answer. Customer order execution, sizing and costs can also differ from the model.

Where did this snapshot come from?

The figures and rows come from NoGuru’s anonymous public track-record endpoint, saved on 1 November 2026 at 12:20:24 UTC. The source channel is Stepgrid. This page is a fixed snapshot and does not refresh automatically.

The reported window ends on 1 November. The latest captured completion is 31 October at 17:17 UTC. Retrieval time is not a verified price-valuation time: the payload does not give a calculation timestamp for the open holdings.

The operator describes a replay of published signals using the buy sizes applied in this snapshot, before fees. These are not necessarily the sizes customers used historically, and the model is not an audited customer statement. No private account or wallet data is used here.
