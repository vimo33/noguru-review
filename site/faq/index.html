What exactly am I subscribing to?

NoGuru supplies the strategy: buy and sell instructions for real coins, sent as signals. Cornix is the separate service that places those orders in your exchange account, so you do not enter them by hand. You connect a sub-account at your exchange that holds only the money for the bot. You never send trading money to NoGuru.

The three roles

What does spot trading mean here?

You buy and hold actual coins in your exchange account, not a contract that tracks their price. Nothing is borrowed. With borrowed money, called leverage, a fall can force a position to close; that cannot happen here. The coins can still fall in value, and the exchange itself remains a risk.

What happens at the exchange

Is this the same as regular DCA?

No. Everyday dollar-cost averaging buys a fixed amount on a calendar, whatever the price. The NoGuru bot reacts to price: it buys in steps as a coin falls and sells once the price is above the average. A lower buy reduces the average cost and commits more money to that coin. The exact rules for choosing coins, levels and sizes are not published.

How much should I put in?

NoGuru suggests about 3,000 USDT or more, so the bot has room to buy in several steps across several coins. Below about 500 USDT it does not work; at about 2,000 USDT it can fill part of its orders.

That is how the bot is sized, not advice about your money. Only use an amount you can leave alone for months.

How long do I have to hold?

There is no fixed period. In the record most trades finish within a day, and a few take weeks or months. A trade ends when its sale goes through or when you close it yourself. A coin can stay below its cost for months, and a recovery may never come.

Can it lose money?

Yes. The bot sells a coin only above its average cost, so a finished cycle is not a loss. The risk sits in the trades that are still open: while a coin is below its average cost it is worth less than you paid, and there is no stop loss. A coin that never recovers would be a real loss. Exchange fees and the two subscriptions also come off your result.

The risks

Why does one of my Cornix trades show a loss?

Cornix allows at most ten buys in one trade. When a coin keeps falling and the bot needs more buys than that, it opens a second Cornix trade for the same coin, and sometimes a third.

The first trade holds the buys made at the highest prices. The bot sells only once the price is above the average of everything it bought, across all the trades of the group. So the later, cheaper trades show a gain, and now and then the first one may show a loss when you look at it alone.

Add the trades of the group together and the result is positive. The bot never sells a group at a loss.

Why buy again when the price falls?

A buy at a lower price reduces the average paid per coin, so the sale can come sooner. It also puts more money into that coin. The sale price can be above the new average and still below your first buy. None of this finds the bottom or guarantees a recovery.

Are published gains what I would earn?

No. The record shows every signal NoGuru published, as a Cornix bot following the channel trades it, before fees. Your start date, your fills, your settings, your exchange and your costs differ. Finished trades also leave out the coins that are still open below cost.

Do I need both NoGuru and Cornix?

Yes, today. NoGuru sends the signals and Cornix places the orders. They are separate companies with separate accounts and separate bills. Cornix’s Advanced plan is enough for the NoGuru bot; Premium is not required.

Do the two 14-day trials start together?

No. The two trials have separate clocks. Cornix’s starts when your first trade is created in Cornix. NoGuru’s start and end dates are shown by the members bot. Check both; starting one does not start or extend the other.

The two free trialsThe setup, step by step

How do NoGuru payments and renewals work?

NoGuru is free for 14 days, once per Telegram account. To continue, you pay a crypto invoice for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months; the published discounts for 3, 6 and 12 months are 10%, 15% and 20%. Nothing renews automatically.

The bot sends a fresh invoice three days before a paid period ends. If you do not pay, there is a 24-hour grace period before channel access ends. The founding price ends if the subscription lapses. The grace period does not extend the free trial.

The payment and renewal rules

Is Cornix’s 48-hour rule the same as NoGuru’s grace period?

No. NoGuru’s 24 hours are about renewing a paid membership. Cornix has its own rule: after a plan or a free trial ends without payment, it keeps managing open trades for 48 hours and then closes them inside Cornix. The coins stay at your exchange. Older Cornix accounts and failed PayPal renewals have separate rules.

Which setup settings do I use?

Use a sub-account that holds only the money for the NoGuru bot. On its API key, allow spot trading and never withdrawals. Join the invited channel with the Telegram account you link to Cornix. Then open NoGuru’s shared bot in Cornix, check the account and choose Create & Activate: your copy takes over NoGuru’s settings.

To set it up by hand instead, create a Cornix Signals Bot that follows the channel. Set the fallback amount to 3%, choose Percentage, tick “Only use if not defined by group”, and leave everything else at its default. The tick lets each signal’s own amount apply; the 3% is only a fallback.

Can NoGuru withdraw my funds?

No. The key you create allows trading with withdrawals switched off, and your money stays at your exchange. That closes one door; it does not remove the risks of the exchange itself or of a key that leaks. Never share the secret part of a key.

Your exchange and your key

Which exchanges can I use?

Cornix connects to several exchanges. NoGuru’s most-tested setup is Binance. For any exchange, check three things: that Cornix supports it for spot trading, that it accepts you in your country, and that the account type works with a sub-account and an API key.

What happens when I stop or cancel?

Stop the bot in Cornix and it places no new orders. Stopping sells nothing: the coins stay in your exchange account and you decide whether to keep or sell them. Ending a subscription or deleting an API key does not sell coins either.

Billing is separate. NoGuru is paid by invoice, so to end it you simply do not pay the next one. A Cornix plan is cancelled in Cornix.

Can I automatically accumulate Bitcoin?

Not through NoGuru. Turning profits into Bitcoin automatically is not part of the offer. You can convert by hand at your exchange; that is your own action, and it leaves less cash for the bot.

Is it completely hands-off?

Mostly. Once it is set up, Cornix places the orders and you approve nothing by hand. It is still worth opening Cornix from time to time to look at your open trades, the cash still free, any alerts, and the dates your subscriptions end. You do not need to watch charts.
