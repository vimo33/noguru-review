About NoGuru

A process you can inspect.
Without a guru to follow.

NoGuru is Florian’s proprietary spot-trading strategy, delivered as instructions through Cornix. The aim is to automate routine order handling while making the process understandable.

Built to spend less time staring at charts.

Florian wanted to automate the repetitive work of watching charts and placing orders. That motivation shapes the service: a plan for the next buy, a target for the sale, and a clear view of what remains exposed.

The routine is automated. The decision stays yours. You still need to understand the setup, monitor it and decide how to manage your exposure.

What NoGuru does

The strategy plans purchases and a target sale. Cornix translates those instructions into orders in each subscriber's exchange account. The proprietary trading logic remains controlled by Florian.

The public website sometimes calls the strategy “Stepgrid.” This website covers the public NoGuru service through Cornix. The explanations and evidence here relate to that Cornix-based service.

What remains yours

You choose whether to participate, select an eligible exchange, configure your account and monitor its operation. Coins and trading funds remain at your exchange, with the risks that entails.
