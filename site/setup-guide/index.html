Setup guide

Understand it first. Then try the workflow.

The setup uses three services: NoGuru for the signals, Cornix to place the orders, and your own exchange account for the money. Here is the order to do it in, and what happens when a trial or a subscription ends.

First, check that you can use the services.

Cornix does not serve every country, and exchanges have their own rules. Check both before you start either trial.

Two trials, two clocks.

NoGuru and Cornix are each free for 14 days, and neither asks for a card. Starting one does not start, reset or extend the other, so write down both end dates.

A free trial is free of subscription fees only. The trades are real: the bot buys real coins with your money, and they can fall in value.

The two free trials

A practical order for getting ready.

These are NoGuru’s published settings for the bot. They are not a personal recommendation about how much money to commit.

  1. Understand the bot and what happens when prices fall. Read how the buys and the sale work, look at the record and follow the worked example. There is no stop loss and no fixed holding period.
  2. Check that you can use the services. Read Cornix’s list of excluded countries and the exchange list. Your exchange account needs spot trading, the coins the bot trades, a sub-account and an API key that Cornix supports. NoGuru’s most-tested setup is Binance; that does not override an exchange’s or a country’s rules.
  3. Start your NoGuru trial and join the channel. Open the members bot, @NoGuruMembers_bot, in Telegram from the home page and tap Start. It sends the invitation to the private channel where the signals are posted. The trial is 14 days, once per Telegram account, with no payment details. Join with the same Telegram account you will link to Cornix, and check your start and end dates in the bot.
  4. Create your Cornix account. You can register by email or with Telegram; link the Telegram account you used for NoGuru. Look at Cornix’s plans and trial rules. Registering does not start Cornix’s trial; your first trade in Cornix does.
  5. Create a sub-account for the NoGuru bot only. Move only the money for the bot into it. Each signal sizes its buy as a percentage of the whole connected account, so other bots or other money in the same account would distort the sizes and could use up the cash meant for the lower buys. Create the API key by your exchange’s instructions: allow spot trading, never withdrawals.
  6. Copy the bot, or enter the settings by hand. To copy, open NoGuru’s shared bot in Cornix from the home page, check that the right exchange account is selected and choose Create & Activate. By hand, create a Signals Bot that follows the NoGuru channel; on its General tab set the fallback amount to 3%, choose Percentage and tick “Only use if not defined by group”; leave every other setting at its default. The tick lets each signal’s own amount apply, so the 3% is only a fallback. Create saves the bot switched off; Create & Activate switches it on for the signals that follow.
  7. Check what actually happens. Look at the selected account, the channel, the cash still free and any alerts. Compare Cornix’s trade view with the orders at your exchange: a bot that is set up is not proof that an order was filled.

Your money. Your deliberate choice.

NoGuru suggests about 3,000 USDT or more, so the bot has room to buy in several steps across several coins. Below about 500 USDT it does not work. This describes how the bot is sized; it is not advice about your money.

Never give anyone permission to withdraw, never share the secret part of an API key in a chat, and never work around an exchange’s country rules.

What 14 days can tell you

  • Whether you understand the workflow and the controls.
  • Whether your setup connects and the signals turn into orders.
  • How the messages, the monitoring and the account views feel to use.
  • Whether the costs and the responsibility fit what you expected.

What 14 days cannot tell you

How it performs over a long time, how long a typical trade takes, how it holds up in different markets, or that even one cycle will finish. A trial can end with every buy still open.

When the NoGuru trial ends

Access ends after 14 days unless you pay or have earned extra free time. Nothing is charged automatically and there is nothing to cancel. Losing access to the channel does not sell coins or cancel orders at your exchange.

When a paid NoGuru period ends

NoGuru does not renew by itself. The bot sends a fresh crypto invoice three days before a paid period ends. If it stays unpaid, there is a 24-hour grace period, then channel access ends. The founding price ends if the subscription lapses; you can rejoin at the price that applies then.

Look at your Cornix bot, your pending orders and your holdings before access ends. Cornix’s own expiry rules are a different matter.

The payment and renewal rules

When the Cornix trial ends

Without a paid plan, Cornix sets the account to inactive but keeps managing existing trades for 48 hours. Then it closes those trades inside Cornix. The coins stay at your exchange; nothing is sold automatically.

When a paid Cornix plan ends

Cornix describes the same 48 hours after a paid plan ends: it manages open trades, then closes them inside Cornix while the coins stay at the exchange. An inactive account cannot open new trades, bots or connections.

Two exceptions: accounts created before 18 February 2026 can fall back to Cornix’s older Free plan with their trades still managed, and a failed PayPal renewal has its own 72 hours for another payment attempt. These are separate cases, not extra time added to every expiry.

So decide in time whether to subscribe, to stop new buys while you manage what you hold, or to close positions yourself. Each has different costs and risks.
