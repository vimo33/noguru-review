A practical order for getting ready.

These are NoGuru’s published operator settings for the NoGuru bot, not a personal recommendation about how much money to commit. You can begin with Cornix’s built-in demo without funding an exchange or creating an API key. The live-account steps below apply when you choose to connect real funds.

  1. Understand the strategy and what happens when prices fall Read how the buys and target work , inspect the published record , and try the favourable and adverse scenarios . The strategy has no stop-loss, and there is no fixed holding period.
  2. Check service eligibility and, for live trading, your exchange Check Cornix’s service terms and the exchange comparison . A live exchange account needs eligible spot trading, the required pairs, sub-account support and an API connection that Cornix supports. NoGuru identifies Binance as its most-tested venue; that does not override exchange or country restrictions.
  3. Request NoGuru access, then join the private channel On NoGuru’s original signup page ↗ , enter your Telegram username. Choose Start with 14 days free , or select a paid term and payment asset. Before submission, the page requires four acknowledgements: The terms and risk disclaimer. The setup instructions, including that different settings produce different results. The risk that holdings remain below cost while waiting for a recovery. NoGuru supplies signals; Cornix’s exchange connection and order execution are separate responsibilities. The published free-access flow then directs you to open @NoGuruMembers_bot ↗ and tap Start for invitation delivery. The paid route creates a crypto invoice; the site says the invitation is sent after payment clears. The pricing page also offers a direct Telegram trial route. Use the bot’s invitation to join the NoGuru channel with the same Telegram account linked to Cornix . The bot also supplies the Academy link. Opening a link alone does not confirm that access has activated. NoGuru gives 14 days free once per Telegram account, without payment details. Check your access start and expiry dates in the NoGuru membership bot. Cornix has a separate trial clock, starting with its first created trade.
  4. Register with Cornix, then choose demo or live Cornix supports email or Telegram registration. In its setup flow, Continue with a Demo Account uses simulated funds; I want to explore instead skips optional setup. The built-in demo needs no exchange or API connection. Cornix does not support connecting external exchange demo accounts or testnets through an API. Link the Telegram account you will use for NoGuru. For a live setup, create and connect the dedicated exchange sub-account described in the next step. Review the official plans and trial rules . Registration alone does not start Cornix’s trial; the first created trade, including a demo or backtest, does. Cornix registration and setup guide ↗
  5. For live trading, create a sub-account for the NoGuru bot only Demo users can skip this exchange step. For live trading, move only the funds allocated to this strategy into that sub-account. Each signal sizes its buy as a percentage of the whole connected account. Other bots or unrelated funds in the same account would affect that sizing and could consume the reserve intended for deeper buys. Use your exchange’s instructions to create the sub-account and its API connection. Enable spot trading; never enable withdrawals. Keep the reserved funds available for the strategy’s lower buy levels.
  6. Copy the bot, or enter the documented settings Copy route: open NoGuru’s published bot in Cornix ↗ , check the selected account and environment, then choose Create & Activate if you intend to enable the copy. Cornix login is required. Select the intended live or demo environment and check the exchange account before making a copy. Manual route: create a Signals Bot following the invited NoGuru channel. On its General tab, set the fallback amount to 3% , choose Percentage , and tick “Only use if not defined by group” . Leave every other Cornix setting at its default. The checkbox lets each signal’s own amount take precedence. The 3% value is a fallback, not an instruction to make every buy 3% of your account. Create saves an inactive bot. Create & Activate enables it to open trades when subsequent signals arrive. For simulated signal following, use a demo Signals Bot . Cornix’s separate One Click Follow feature is live-only; it is not the demo bot-copy route. Read Cornix’s Signals Bot controls ↗
  7. Review the setup and monitor what actually happens Check the selected account, channel, spot pairs, available reserve and alerts. Compare Cornix’s trade view with orders and fills at your exchange. A configured bot is not proof that a trade filled. Demo activity is simulated; it does not verify live execution.

What 14 days can tell you

  • Whether you understand the workflow and controls.
  • Whether the chosen setup connects and processes signals as intended.
  • How monitoring, messages and account views feel to use.
  • Whether the costs and responsibility fit your expectations.

What 14 days cannot establish

Reliable long-term performance, a typical holding period, resilience through different markets, or a guarantee that even one cycle will complete.

Free access and paid renewals

NoGuru free-access expiry

The 14-day access ends unless you pay or have earned additional free access. There is no automatic charge or cancellation to arrange. Channel access ending does not automatically sell coins or cancel exchange orders.

Paid NoGuru renewal

NoGuru does not renew automatically. The bot sends a fresh crypto invoice three days before a paid period expires. If it remains unpaid, the renewal rules allow a 24-hour grace period, then channel access ends. The founding rate ends if the subscription lapses; you can rejoin at the rate then available.

The 24-hour renewal grace is separate from the free trial. Inspect your Cornix bot, pending orders and holdings before access ends; do not apply Cornix’s subscription-expiry rules to NoGuru channel removal.

Cornix free-trial expiry

Without a paid plan, Cornix makes the account inactive but continues managing existing trades for 48 hours. It then closes those trades within Cornix; they remain open at the exchange. This is not an automatic market sale. Cornix’s separately free demo remains available.

Paid Cornix expiry

For current accounts, Cornix documents the same 48-hour management window after a paid subscription expires, followed by internal closure while positions remain at the exchange. Inactive accounts cannot create new trades, bots or connections.

Separate exceptions apply: accounts created before 18 February 2026 can return to the legacy Free plan with existing trades still managed. A failed PayPal renewal has its own 72-hour payment-retry grace. These are different cases, not extra time to add to every expiry. See Cornix’s paid-expiry guide and billing rules .

Decide whether to subscribe, stop new entries while managing existing holdings, or close positions yourself. Each has different costs and risks.
