Your exchange: the account itself

For identity checks, deposits, withdrawals or access to your account, use the support inside your exchange’s official app or website.

Never send anyone a password, the secret part of an API key or a seed phrase, and never give anyone permission to withdraw.

Recognise official NoGuru contacts.

NoGuru’s official places are noguru.io, the Telegram channels linked from it and the members bot, @NoGuruMembers_bot. NoGuru never messages you first, never asks for keys or seed phrases, and never offers to manage your account.

So start from the links on this site. A message that claims to be from NoGuru is no reason to share anything or hand over control of an account.
