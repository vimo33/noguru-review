Market context is a separate source

The market page reads a timestamped trend matrix. Its readings describe where prices sit relative to recent averages, in units of typical movement. They are not percentage returns, strategy fills or buying instructions.

Recent market-drop episodes on the original dashboard are market observations. Pricing’s historical crash replays are a different set of strategy scenarios. Neither is a customer account.

Published signal history

The Evidence view requests the public signal feed and shows its coverage window, fetch status and stated before-fee basis. It contains completed-cycle rows and open aggregates, not customer balances.

The server’s exact allocation normalization and a current monetary account reconciliation are not exposed. If the source cannot be reached, the page says so; no remembered figure silently replaces it.
