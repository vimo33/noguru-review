What the $49 pays for

NoGuru picks the coins, plans each purchase and sets each sale, and keeps reviewing all of it as the market changes. You do not plan orders yourself.

The founding price is $49 a month while founding places last. The standard price is $99 a month. These are two alternative prices, never two charges. Confirm the current offer before paying.

How it works

How much money makes the subscription pay for itself

Take the monthly price and divide it by the monthly pace of the public record. The answer is the amount of trading money that would earn exactly the subscription in a month at that pace. It is rounded up to the next $10, so it never understates what you need.

Below that amount you would pay more than the bot earns for you. Above it, the difference is yours. The figure moves as the record moves, so it is read from the live record each time you open this page.

Where it runs today: Cornix

NoGuru decides what to buy and sell. Cornix is the tool that places those orders in your exchange account. It is a separate company, so you set up and pay for its plan with Cornix directly, not here.

New Cornix users get 14 days free with no payment details. After that, Cornix Advanced costs $49.90 a month, or $32.99 a month when billed yearly. Check that the plan supports your exchange before you choose.

Cornix is the first place NoGuru runs. Other platforms are planned, and this page will list them when they are ready.

Cornix does not serve every country. Its terms prohibit use by citizens and residents of: United States, Canada, Colombia, Ecuador, Bolivia; United Kingdom, Russia, Turkey, North Macedonia; Israel, Iran, Lebanon, Syria, Egypt, Algeria, Morocco; China, Vietnam, Nepal, North Korea. They also exclude any country under country-wide sanctions and any place whose law prohibits the service. The terms say “among others”, so this list is a minimum. If you live in one of these places you cannot run NoGuru through Cornix today.

Cornix also does not support some regional exchange sites: Binance.US, okx.eu, okx.us and Bybit.eu.

Your trading money is not a fee

It stays in your own exchange account the whole time. NoGuru suggests about 3,000 USDT or more, so the bot has room to buy in several steps across several coins. Your exchange charges its normal trading fees on each order.

Below 500 USDT the bot does not work. At about 2,000 USDT it can fill part of its orders; above 3,000 USDT it can fill most of them. A larger amount gives the bot more room; it does not guarantee a higher percentage or a recovery.
