Why win rate is not the score

How often a trade wins matters together with how much it wins or loses.

Two hypothetical methods

AssumptionMethod AMethod B
Win frequency40%70%
Average winning trade+$250+$50
Average losing trade−$100−$100
Arithmetic average per trade before costs+$40+$5

For A: (0.40 × $250) − (0.60 × $100) = $40. For B: (0.70 × $50) − (0.30 × $100) = $5. These invented inputs explain the formula; they are not NoGuru estimates or predictions.

Count what remains open

An average of completed trades can look attractive while the portfolio holds substantial losses that have not been sold. Trading fees and subscriptions also matter. A useful evaluation includes open holdings, total equity, cash flows and the period observed.

How this relates to NoGuru

The public NoGuru record groups buys into completed cycles. A cycle percentage is not the same as a return on the full account. Do not apply a stop-based “R” score to NoGuru as though its loss were capped.
