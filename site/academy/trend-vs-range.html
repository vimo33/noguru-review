Trends, ranges and changing conditions

A strategy’s assumptions can fit one period and fail in another.

← All lessons

Two descriptive patterns

A trend describes sustained directional movement over a chosen window. A range describes repeated movement within an area. The classification depends on the time period and may be clear only after the event.

Understand the failure path

General approachWhat can go wrong
Buying after falls and seeking recoveryPrice continues lower or never recovers
Following a breakoutThe move reverses repeatedly
Assuming a stable rangeThe market leaves the range and stays outside it

A label is not a forecast

The same price series can appear to trend on one timeframe and range on another. An indicator may describe recent conditions without identifying the next regime.

How this relates to NoGuru

NoGuru’s averaging and target-sale logic depends on a sufficient recovery to complete a cycle. That recovery may not arrive.
