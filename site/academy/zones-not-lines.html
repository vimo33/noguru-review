Why a zone can be useful

Prior highs, lows and round numbers provide shared reference points. Orders and decisions need not cluster at one exact price. Describing a region can avoid giving a single tick more precision than the evidence supports.

What the drawing cannot establish

An apparent support zone can fail completely. Passing a previous high does not establish continuation, and briefly crossing a low does not prove manipulation or a forthcoming rebound.

Keep examples honest

A schematic can show how an order works without claiming that the real market will follow its line. Label it as illustrative, show adverse paths and avoid drawing every buy at a bottom or every sale at a top.

How this relates to NoGuru

NoGuru’s planned buys and target sales are instructions, not predictions of market bottoms and tops. Actual fills depend on execution and available liquidity.
