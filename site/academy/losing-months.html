Academy · Evaluation & habits

Evaluating a difficult month

A positive closed-trade number can coexist with a falling account.

← All lessons

Use the whole-account question

What was the account worth at the start and end, after adjusting for deposits and withdrawals? Include the market value of open holdings and all relevant costs. Completed profits alone cannot answer that question.

Separate operation from outcome

Did orders execute as intended? Were settings changed? Did fees or minimum sizes affect fills? These operational facts should be reviewed alongside market losses, rather than hidden behind a headline percentage.

Changing the rules changes the comparison

Skipping a signal, adding an extra trade, changing order sizes or selling manually changes the sequence your account follows. Log the change and its time. A modified account can still be reviewed, but its result is not a like-for-like test of the original signal model.

For example, a record that excludes one scheduled entry should identify that omission rather than compare its total as though every signal had been taken. Different fills, fees and start times also affect comparability even when no deliberate change was made.

Apply the same scrutiny after a winning month. A gain does not prove an override helped; a loss does not by itself prove the unchanged method failed. This is a point about interpreting evidence, not a demand to take every trade or keep subscribing.

The original lesson’s hypothetical monthly arithmetic

For the invented independent model with a 40% win chance and fixed +2R/−1R outcomes before costs, a 20-trade total is 3K − 20, where K is the number of wins. A loss means at most six wins. The exact binomial probability is 25.0011%, approximately one in four.

The source calls this “exactly” one in four; the displayed value here is calculated rather than treated as an exact quarter. Two independent losing 20-trade windows have a probability of about 6.25% under the same assumptions. This does not predict calendar months: real trade counts, returns and market conditions vary, and trades can be dependent. It estimates nothing about NoGuru.

No required holding period

There is no universal number of weeks that makes an evaluation fair or a losing holding safe. A strategy can stop fitting your circumstances, fail operationally or lose money. Decide with the actual exposure and evidence in view.

How this relates to NoGuru

NoGuru subscriptions and exchange holdings have separate lifecycles. Cancelling a subscription does not automatically sell coins or make their market risk disappear.
