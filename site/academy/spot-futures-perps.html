Academy · Market fundamentals

Spot, futures and perpetuals

Similar-looking charts can represent very different instruments.

← All lessons

Know what the account holds

InstrumentWhat it representsImportant differences
SpotThe purchased crypto assetNo contract expiry; price and custody risk remain
Dated futureA derivative contract with an expirySettlement, collateral and margin rules apply
Perpetual futureA derivative without a fixed expiryFunding payments and margin/liquidation rules may apply

The symbol is not enough

An exchange may offer spot and perpetual markets for the same asset. Verify the market type, account type, quote asset and permitted order actions. “BTC” on a screen does not by itself identify which instrument you are using.

How this relates to NoGuru

NoGuru’s public strategy is spot, without borrowing or leverage. Futures support in Cornix’s exchange list does not establish that an exchange supports NoGuru’s required spot setup.
