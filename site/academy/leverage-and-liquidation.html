Academy · Loss & recovery

Leverage and liquidation

Borrowed exposure amplifies both gains and losses relative to the money posted.

← All lessons

What changes with leverage

In a simplified example, $1,000 of margin at 10× controls $10,000 of exposure. A 5% adverse move is a $500 loss before fees: half the original margin. The market move is 5%; the loss relative to the margin is 50%.

LeverageAdverse move equal to initial margin, before other effects
2×50%
5×20%
10×10%
20×5%

Liquidation can happen earlier

The simple calculation is not a liquidation-price formula. Maintenance margin, fees, collateral rules and other positions affect the exchange’s threshold. An exchange may close the position before the initial margin has been fully consumed.

How this relates to NoGuru

Public NoGuru uses spot without leverage. That avoids leveraged liquidation in the intended configuration; it does not protect the price of the coins or the funds held at an exchange.
