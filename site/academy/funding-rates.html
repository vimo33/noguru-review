Academy · Market fundamentals

Funding payments in perpetual markets

A small recurring percentage can become a material holding cost.

← All lessons

Follow the payment

Perpetual venues use funding mechanisms to help align derivative and spot prices. Depending on the rate, one side pays the other. Rates, intervals and formulas vary by contract and venue; do not assume every market settles every eight hours.

An arithmetic example

A hypothetical 0.01% charge on a $10,000 position is $1 for that interval. If the same rate applied three times, the cost would be $3. The next rate can change or reverse; multiplying today’s rate into a year is not a reliable forecast.

Context is not an instruction

Funding describes one aspect of positioning and carrying cost. Positive funding does not guarantee a fall, and negative funding does not guarantee a rally.

How this relates to NoGuru

NoGuru spot holdings do not incur perpetual funding in the intended setup. Exchange trading fees and separate subscription bills still apply.
