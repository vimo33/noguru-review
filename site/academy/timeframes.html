Academy · Charts & evidence

One market, several timeframes

A short-term fall can sit inside a longer-term rise.

← All lessons

Both views can be true

An illustrative coin rises from $80 to $100 over a month and then falls from $100 to $95 in a day. The monthly change remains positive while the day is negative. The charts answer different questions.

Keep the measurement consistent

State the start, end, interval and price source when comparing charts. Changing the window after seeing the outcome can select a flattering view. An indicator calculated from hourly bars is not interchangeable with one calculated from daily bars.

Do not change the question to fit a loss

A longer chart cannot remove the money currently lost. Record the intended evaluation horizon and operational limits, then reassess explicitly when facts change.

How this relates to NoGuru

A 14-day NoGuru trial can show setup and operation. It cannot provide reliable evidence of long-term performance or ensure that an open cycle finishes.
