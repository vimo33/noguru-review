What does recovery take?

The gain needed after a loss. A calculation of the hurdle, never a prediction that an asset will clear it.

42.86% gain needed

An illustrative $1,000 becomes $700.00 after the loss. Returning to $1,000 requires a $300.00 gain.

The formula

Required gain = loss ÷ (1 − loss), with percentages expressed as decimals.

For 30%: 0.30 ÷ 0.70 = 0.4286, or 42.86%.

Time is deliberately absent

No monthly return is assumed. The calculation cannot tell you whether recovery happens, how long it takes or whether to hold. At a 100% loss, no percentage gain on the zero balance can restore it.

Fees, deposits and withdrawals are excluded. $1,000 is an arithmetic example, not a suggested allocation.

For NoGuru, the open loss still matters.

No stop-loss is used. Further buys can lower average cost while increasing committed capital. They cannot guarantee recovery.
