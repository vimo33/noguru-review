Academy · Charts & evidence

What chart analysis can and cannot do

Charts describe observations. Useful rules still need evidence.

← All lessons

Make a decision reviewable

A defined rule can specify a price condition, an order and a measurement window. This makes the decision testable. It does not, by itself, establish a profitable edge.

Look for a fair test

A credible historical test explains data coverage, costs, assumptions, how parameters were selected, and whether the test used information unavailable at the time. Trying many rules and showing only the winner can exaggerate the evidence.

Ask what is missing

Look for rejected trades, open holdings, market conditions that differ from the test, and what the rule does when the expected move fails. Consistency makes a method easier to inspect; it does not guarantee a good result.

How this relates to NoGuru

NoGuru’s trading rules are designed and maintained by its operator. The public signal model, a historical replay and a customer account are different evidence types. Read the methodology before comparing them.
