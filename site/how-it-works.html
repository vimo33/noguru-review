How NoGuru works

A trading plan.
With the moving parts explained.

NoGuru is a paid strategy and signal service for buying and selling crypto coins. Cornix, a separate automated trading platform, places NoGuru’s orders in your exchange account, where your funds stay. You connect a dedicated account and follow the documented settings. The intended strategy uses spot holdings, without leverage or a stop-loss.

The trading mechanism

Buy in steps.
Aim for a recovery.

The strategy responds to price conditions with staged buys and target sales. It is different from buying a fixed amount on a calendar, often called dollar-cost averaging (DCA). The exact selection, timing and sizing rules remain proprietary.

A schematic, not a forecast or trade history. Targets relate to the combined holding’s average cost; prices and waiting times vary.

One possible path

The price reaches a sale target.

Each cycle groups purchases of a coin with the sale intended to close the combined holding. Lower buys can reduce its average cost. A sale may then happen above that average while still below the first purchase price. Fees reduce any gain.

After a completed sale, proceeds may be used for later trades. The next cycle has its own prices and timing; its first buy need not be below the previous sale.

Another possible path

The price keeps falling.

Additional purchases use more cash and increase exposure. There is no stop-loss to close the holding at a defined loss. The coin can remain below cost for a long time, fall much further, or never recover to the target.

Open holdings tie up capital. There is no guaranteed exit, recovery, repeat cycle or maximum waiting time.

What sits behind the signals

What’s included in your subscription

NoGuru access covers an ongoing strategy, not just a bot setting copied once. The published Stepgrid approach combines coin selection, individual trading plans and adjustments as markets change.

NoGuru supplies those instructions. Cornix is the separate service that places orders, and your exchange holds the funds. The exact selection, sizing and tuning logic remains proprietary.

Strategy description: NoGuru’s published overview and subscription explanation, checked 1 November 2026. These describe the operator’s process, not an assurance of performance.

  1. Coins selected and reviewed by rules.

    The strategy selects coins using rules and re-checks that selection every night.

  2. Price-level entries and trend-based sizing.

    Buys wait at planned lower price levels. Each buy’s size responds to the market’s trend, rather than using a fixed calendar purchase.

  3. A reserve for deeper buys.

    Part of the allocation is held back for lower steps in the plan. Spending that reserve elsewhere changes the funds available when a deeper buy arrives.

  4. A separate ladder and exit for each coin.

    Several coins can have active plans at once. Each has its own sequence of buys and target for selling the combined holding. Reaching that target is not guaranteed.

  5. Ongoing tuning as conditions change.

    NoGuru re-tunes coin-specific plans as markets change and can retire coins from the strategy. This continuing work is part of the subscription; it does not ensure that a coin recovers or a trade succeeds.

Who does what

Three services.
Your account.

Spot means holding the actual coins at your exchange, rather than a leveraged price contract. Without borrowing or margin, this setup avoids forced closure caused by losses on a leveraged position. Coin values can still fall severely. Your trading capital stays at the exchange, not with NoGuru.

Trade permission is powerful.

Withdrawal permission should remain disabled in the intended setup. That does not remove trading losses, API misuse, security incidents or exchange failure. Never share your login, seed phrase or API secret in a support conversation.

What automation changes

Less manual order entry.
A setup you still oversee.

  1. You choose whether the approach fits.

    Understand the absence of a stop-loss, the possibility of long holds, the separate bills and the limits of the published record before allocating anything.

  2. You set up the connection.

    Use a dedicated Stepgrid-only sub-account with spot trading enabled and withdrawals disabled. Join the private channel with the Telegram account linked to Cornix, then copy the operator’s bot or apply its documented Signals Bot settings. Follow the setup sequence and exact fallback settings.

  3. You keep an eye on operation.

    Cornix handles routine order submission. You check whether orders actually fill, connections remain active and reserves suit the chosen setup. Review open holdings, costs and expiry dates; automation does not remove those decisions.

  4. You decide when to change or stop.

    Stopping new instructions, cancelling a subscription and selling an existing holding are different actions. Review open orders and positions at your exchange before making changes.

Where does the profit go?

A completed spot sale produces proceeds in the exchange account. Automatic conversion of profits to Bitcoin is not a verified feature of the public NoGuru service.

Any separate conversion is your action at the exchange and can affect the funds available for future trades.
