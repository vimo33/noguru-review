Getting started

Understand it first.
Then try the workflow.

NoGuru gives access to its trading instructions. Cornix provides the tools that can place orders at your exchange. Each advertises a separate 14-day trial. Their clocks, costs and expiry rules are different.

Educational information, not financial advice. A free subscription trial does not make live trading risk-free.

First, check service eligibility.

Cornix service restrictions are broader than a promotional offer. Check country and account eligibility before activating either service.

A practical order
for getting ready.

Move through the three services deliberately. You can explore this website and the examples without opening an account or connecting an exchange.

  1. Understand the strategy and the downside

    Read how the buys and target work, inspect the published record, and try the favourable and adverse scenarios. Be comfortable with no stop-loss and an uncertain holding period.

  2. Check your exchange and country

    Check Cornix’s service terms and the full exchange comparison. Confirm the exact legal exchange entity, spot API access, supported pairs, regional availability and the NoGuru configuration. A Cornix logo does not establish all of these.

  3. Prepare Cornix before starting its clock

    Review the official plans and trial rules. A first demo or backtest can begin the trial. Note its expiry date.

  4. Activate NoGuru in its official Telegram bot

    Confirm your NoGuru trial eligibility, start and end dates, current price, and any already-earned access. The published bot is @NoGuruMembers_bot ↗. Opening the link does not activate anything in this preview; the bot's own actions and terms apply.

  5. Configure a demo or an eligible exchange connection

    Follow the official setup. Check whether the chosen NoGuru channel and settings work with Cornix's demo mode; a generic demo is not proof of live execution. For a real connection, use the dedicated subaccount required by NoGuru’s published setup, with only required trading permissions and withdrawals disabled. Any account alternative needs explicit configuration confirmation.

  6. Check settings and monitor what actually happens

    Verify allocation, order sizes, channel, spot market, pairs, available reserve and alerts. Compare Cornix's trade view with actual orders at your exchange. A configured bot is not proof that a trade filled.

What 14 days can tell you

  • Whether you understand the workflow and controls.
  • Whether the chosen setup connects and processes signals as intended.
  • How monitoring, messages and account views feel to use.
  • Whether the costs and responsibility fit your expectations.

What 14 days cannot establish

Reliable long-term performance, a typical holding period, resilience through different markets, or a guarantee that even one cycle will complete.

Before either trial expires

Open holdings need a plan
even if you do not subscribe.

NoGuru expiry

Strategy access ends without payment or an applicable entitlement. Losing signal access does not automatically close your holdings. Confirm whether instructions for existing trades will continue. Inspect the actual bot, pending orders and exchange positions before expiry.

Cornix live-trial expiry

Cornix's guide says existing trades are managed for a further 48 hours after trial expiry. Then Cornix ends its internal trade tracking. The assets and relevant exchange positions can remain; this is not an automatic market sale. Cornix’s separate free demo can remain available; access to NoGuru’s channel is a different entitlement.

Decide whether to subscribe, stop new entries while managing existing holdings, or close positions yourself. Each has different costs and risks.
