    Ask me anything

    The five questions everyone asks.

    Is my money safe?It stays in your own exchange account. The bot can trade it. It can’t withdraw it.

    You connect Cornix to your exchange with a key that allows trading only; withdrawals stay switched off. Use a separate sub-account that holds just the money you want the bot to trade. You never paste a key into this site.

    What that doesn’t protect you from: the market itself, and the exchange that holds your account.

    Go deeper: How it worksExchangesWhat an API key is

    What happens in a crash?It buys in steps on the way down, then holds and waits. It doesn’t sell in a panic.

    There is no stop loss. Coins it holds can lose value for a long time, and some may never come back. If the cash set aside runs out, buying pauses until a sale frees some up.

    NoGuru’s own replay of a market top like October 2025 shows the account worth 48% less at market prices at its lowest point.
