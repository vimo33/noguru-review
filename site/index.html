Ask me anything

The six questions everyone asks.

Is my money safe?It stays in your own exchange account. The bot can trade it. It can’t withdraw it.

You connect Cornix to your exchange with a key that allows trading only; withdrawals stay switched off. Use a separate sub-account that holds just the money you want the bot to trade. You never paste a key into this site.

What that doesn’t protect you from: the market itself, and the exchange that holds your account.

What happens in a crash?It buys in steps on the way down, then holds and waits. It doesn’t sell in a panic.

There is no stop loss. Coins it holds can lose value for a long time, and some may never come back. If the cash set aside runs out, buying pauses until a sale frees some up.

How much do I need?About 3,000 USDT or more lets the bot fill most of its orders.

Below 500 USDT it doesn’t work. At about 2,000 USDT it can fill part of the orders. Above 3,000 USDT it can fill most of them, because there is room to buy in several steps across several coins.

Why does one of my Cornix trades show a loss?A long run of buys is split into several Cornix trades. Added up they end in profit, but the first one can show a loss on its own.

Cornix allows at most ten buys in one trade. When a coin keeps falling and the bot needs more buys than that, it opens a second Cornix trade for the same coin, and sometimes a third.

The first trade holds the buys made at the highest prices. The bot sells only once the price is above the average of everything it bought, across all the trades of the group. So the later, cheaper trades show a gain, and now and then the first one may show a loss when you look at it alone.

Add the trades of the group together and the result is positive. The bot never sells a group at a loss.
