Built around a repeatable process.

A trading plan is more than choosing a coin. There are price levels to watch, orders to place and open holdings to keep track of. NoGuru brings those routine steps into one strategy that Cornix carries out in your own exchange account.

The routine is automated. The decision stays yours: you choose whether to take part, how much money to set aside, and when to stop.

What NoGuru does

The bot buys a coin in steps at planned price levels and sets a sale for the combined holding. Its buys follow price, not a weekly or monthly schedule. Cornix turns those instructions into orders in each subscriber’s exchange account.

NoGuru does not hold your money, does not manage your account and does not give personal advice.

How it works

What remains yours

You choose an exchange that accepts you, set up the account and keep an eye on it. Your coins and your trading money stay at your exchange, in your own name.

The bot uses no borrowed money and has no stop loss. Coins it holds can lose value and can stay unsold for a long time.

The risks, said plainly

How to judge it

Understand the mechanism: how each buy changes the average price and how much money is committed.

Read the whole picture: finished trades, coins still held, costs and the limits of the record belong together.

Then decide for yourself whether the setup and the commitment suit you.
