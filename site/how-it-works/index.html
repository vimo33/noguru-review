NoGuru selects and reviews coins, plans purchase sizes and entry levels, and sets sale targets. The ongoing review informs future purchases as conditions change. You do not have to plan every order yourself.

Cornix carries out the configured instructions. Your cash and coins stay in your exchange account. A guide or dashboard is not itself an exchange connection.

The strategy aims for sales above a cycle’s average cost. The supplier describes a trailing exit: once eligible, it follows a rise and seeks a sale after a retreat. A target is an aim; a fill or profit is not guaranteed.

A completed sale releases proceeds that may fund another cycle. The same coin can be considered again if it remains approved and the rules and available cash allow.

Each cycle stays separate. A new cycle does not change the older cycle’s average; holdings can fall and tie up capital.

Several coins can be active at once: one buying, one approaching a target, another waiting below cost. Reserves change as purchases and sales happen. There is finite buying capacity; if available cash is committed, new buying must wait.

A separate cycle may begin lower while an older one remains open. Each has its own average cost. Starting a new cycle does not change the old cycle’s average.

Keep the intended allocation separate

The published setup calls for a dedicated exchange subaccount holding only the capital set aside for the strategy. This keeps percentage-based instructions separate from other funds or bots.

Before activating anything, confirm exchange and Cornix-plan compatibility and use spot-trading API permissions with withdrawals disabled. Never paste credentials into this site. It remains unconnected.

Your judgment still matters

Coins can keep falling or never recover. There is no strategy stop-loss. Pausing new trades does not sell existing holdings or settle open orders; those need a separate decision. Check the account and execution as well as the signals.
