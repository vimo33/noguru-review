Completed profit counts only trades that have been sold. The trades still open are counted separately below, at where they stand today.

Why 2 closed below cost: The bot never sells a cycle at a loss. Cornix allows at most ten buys in one trade, so a longer cycle is split into several Cornix trades. One trade of such a group can show a loss on its own; the group as a whole, which is the cycle, never does.

Still open

The 20 trades still open hold 138.0% of the money set aside, and together stand −25.8% against what they cost, at the latest price.

This is where the risk sits. There is no stop loss: open trades wait for their price, they can fall further, and some may not come back. The share can be above 100% because the same money cycles through several trades over time. Open trades are shown only as this one total, never one by one.

Mostly small wins. Some big ones.
