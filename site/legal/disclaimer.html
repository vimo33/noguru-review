Risk disclosure

Trading losses are possible, including a loss of most or all allocated capital.

No stop-loss. No guaranteed recovery.

NoGuru is an unleveraged spot strategy with no stop-loss. Planned buys can commit more money as a coin falls. Positions may stay below cost for months or indefinitely. A target sale may never execute.

Automation has practical limits

APIs, signals, software, account settings and exchanges can fail. Orders can be delayed, rejected or filled at different prices. Trading-only API permissions do not make an account risk-free. You remain responsible for monitoring and decisions.

Evidence is not a forecast

Published signal models, historical replays, operator accounts and subscriber results are distinct. Gross completed profit excludes open-holding losses and costs. A short trial does not establish long-term performance.

Stopping does not sell everything

Pausing automation, cancelling access or revoking a key does not automatically liquidate existing holdings. Review positions and orders at your exchange. Read the full risk and stopping guide →

Educational information, not financial advice

Site examples explain mechanics and arithmetic, with assumptions. They do not recommend an investment amount, a venue, a personal trade or a holding period. Eligibility and final legal terms require separate review.
