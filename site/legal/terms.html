Terms & subscription status

The product and cost information is reviewable. The final contract still needs a verified provider.

Service boundary

NoGuru provides spot-trading instructions through Cornix. Cornix and your exchange are separate services with their own terms. Trading funds remain with the exchange. No personalised return, recovery or holding period is promised.

Subscription structure

NoGuru offers a 14-day trial once per Telegram account. The first 100 subscribers pay $49 USD/month and keep that rate while their subscription continues without a break. Later new subscribers pay $99 USD/month. A lapsed subscription does not guarantee the earlier rate on return. Your membership checkout shows the applicable rate and payment date; Cornix is separate.

The existing terms describe manual crypto renewal, access ending without payment, and non-refundable payments. Applicable legal cancellation and refund rights need to be reflected in the final contract. Existing earned promotions and credits remain relevant to your membership.

What must be finalised

Correct provider identity; eligible countries and customer scope; definitive trial start and expiry handling; open-trade responsibilities when access ends; payment and renewal processes; legally applicable cancellation/refund rights; support route; dispute and governing-law terms. The final contract must identify the verified provider.

No contract in this preview

There is no checkout, acceptance form or account creation here. Links to existing services remain subject to those services' current terms. This review is not a customer launch or a completed contract.
