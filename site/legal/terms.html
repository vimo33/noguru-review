Terms & subscription status

The product and cost information is reviewable. The final contract still needs a verified provider.

Service boundary

NoGuru provides spot-trading instructions through Cornix. Cornix and your exchange are separate services with their own terms. Trading funds remain with the exchange. No personalised return, recovery or holding period is promised.

Subscription structure

NoGuru offers 14 days free once per Telegram account, with no payment details required. Access ends after 14 days unless you pay or have earned additional access. The first 100 subscribers pay $49 USD/month and keep that rate while their subscription continues without a break. Later new subscribers pay $99 USD/month. The founding rate ends if your subscription lapses; you can rejoin at the rate then available. Your membership checkout shows the applicable rate and covered period; Cornix is separate.

Choose 1, 3, 6 or 12 months. The published longer-term discounts are 10%, 15% and 20% for 3, 6 and 12 months respectively. Payment is by crypto invoice and buys the stated access period upfront; it does not renew automatically.

The bot sends a fresh renewal invoice three days before expiry. If you do not renew a paid subscription, a 24-hour grace period applies before channel access ends. To stop renewing, do not pay the next invoice. This paid-renewal grace period does not extend the free trial. Read the renewal explanation →

The published terms state that payments are non-refundable. Applicable legal cancellation and refund rights need to be reflected in the final contract. Existing earned promotions and credits remain relevant to your membership.

What must be finalised

Correct provider identity; eligible countries and customer scope; handling of updates to existing trades after channel access ends; legally applicable cancellation/refund rights; dispute and governing-law terms. The final contract must identify the verified provider.
