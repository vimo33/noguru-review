Privacy information

This hosted review preview does not ask for account information, wallet data or API keys.

How this preview behaves

The FAQ guide and scenario explorer run in your browser. Questions, example amounts and scenario selections are not submitted to an AI service or server. The preview has no signup, contact form, payment handler or analytics integration.

The logo comparison can keep a local visual preference in browser storage. Use the comparison page to reset it. Page requests pass through hosting and network services and may generate request metadata or logs. Their exact retention settings have not been confirmed for this review link. The built site uses local assets rather than third-party font or chart requests.

External links

Opening Telegram, Cornix, an exchange or another external website takes you to that service. Its own privacy practices and account requirements apply. This preview does not pass the FAQ conversation or scenario values to those links.

Before a customer launch

Confirm the controller's identity and contact details, hosting and log retention, actual processors, lawful purposes and bases, data retention, international transfers, rights process and any cookies or analytics actually used. These facts have not been invented here.

The privacy notice must be completed for a customer launch and its jurisdictions. These review notes describe this preview; they do not describe the processing of other NoGuru sites.
