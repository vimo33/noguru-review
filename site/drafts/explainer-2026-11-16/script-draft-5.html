Full script, draft 5 (main film)

Changed and new lines are highlighted.

01 · The market never closes

The crypto market never closes. Not at night, not on Sunday, not while you're at work. Prices rise and fall around the clock. And most of it happens while you're not looking.

02 · Why doing it by hand is hard

Trading that by hand is hard. You watch a chart. You buy late. The price drops, and you sell at the bottom to make it stop. And losses weigh more than they look: lose half, and you have to double what's left just to get back.

So most people end up following someone who sounds sure. A guru.

03 · Meet the bot

I'm the NoGuru bot. I run by myself, twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week. I keep watch on more than five hundred coins. From those I pick about twenty-five established ones, and those are the ones I trade. For each one I decide three things: when to buy, how much, and when to sell. changed

I don't guess where prices are going. I decide what to do when they move. I buy a coin in steps as it gets cheaper, I sell when it's back above what I paid on average, and I start again.

I don't get tired. I don't get scared. I don't get greedy.

04 · One coin, properly

Let me show you one. I buy a little.

The price falls. I don't panic. I buy a little more, cheaper.

It falls again. I buy again. And again.

None of that was improvised. Every buy was planned: the price, and the size.

Every buy pulls my average cost down.

Here's the part people miss. I don't need the old price back. I only need the price a little above my average.

About two percent above it, I get ready to sell. If the price keeps climbing, I follow it up, and I sell when the rise runs out. Often that's just over two percent. Sometimes it's far more.

The cash comes back with its profit. And the coin is still below where I started.

Sometimes one buy is enough. In my record, about half of all finished cycles needed only one. Sometimes it takes many. There is a fixed limit for each coin, and a fixed total across all of them.

05 · Grid bots, DCA bots, and me

You may know grid bots and D C A bots.

A grid bot trades inside a price range you choose. When the price leaves that range, it stops.

A D C A bot follows one coin down with settings you choose, and sells at a fixed target.

With those bots, the strategy is your job. With me, it's mine. You don't set a range, a spacing or a target. The settings are maintained for you. I work across about twenty-five coins at once, and coins are added and retired by rule. And my sale follows the price up, instead of stopping at a fixed number. changed

All you decide is how much money to set aside.

06 · Zoom out

Now zoom out. I'm not doing this once. I'm doing it on many coins at the same time.

Nobody presses a button. While you work, while you sleep, I keep going, one coin after another, without a break.

Each finished cycle is small. But a small gain, taken often, with the cash going straight back to work, adds up.

Since the first of August I've finished more than a thousand cycles. About four in five were done within a day.

07 · When a coin keeps falling

And when a coin keeps falling? I don't sell it at a loss. I hold it, and I wait for the price to come back above my average.

That can take a long time, and a coin may never come back. Until it does, it's worth less than I paid. So this is for money you can leave alone for months. changed

08 · The record

You don't have to take my word for it. Everything I publish goes into a public record: every finished cycle, and the ones still open.

From the first of August to mid-November, completed profit was thirty-four point nine percent on the money set aside, before fees.

That is finished cycles only. Twenty more are still open, and right now they are worth less than I paid. Count those in at market prices, and on the sixteenth of November the whole pot stood about three percent below where it started. new

It's a record of my signals, not of anyone's account. And the past doesn't promise the future.

09 · Yours to try

Your money stays in your own exchange account, in your name. I can trade it. I can't withdraw it.

You set up the trading once. After that, I do the work, and you check in when you like. changed

Try me free for fourteen days. No card.

You won't get rich in two weeks. You'll see the rhythm: small buys, a few coins waiting, sales coming back. Then you decide.

No guru. No guessing. I just do the work. new
