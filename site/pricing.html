Pricing

Two services.
One clear cost picture.

NoGuru provides the trading instructions. Cornix places the orders in your exchange account. Each service has its own subscription; exchange fees and the money used for trades are separate.

All subscription prices below are in US dollars (USD).

01 / NoGuru

$49 USD / month

For the first 100 subscribers

Keep the $49 monthly rate for as long as your subscription continues without a break. If you let it lapse, this rate is not guaranteed when you return.

Later new subscribers pay $99 USD/month. Your membership checkout shows which rate applies to you.

02 / Cornix

$49.90 USD / month

Advanced, on monthly billing

Cornix Advanced includes three active Signals Bots. Its annual option costs $395.88 upfront, equivalent to $32.99 a month. NoGuru is billed separately.

Cornix has its own service eligibility rules, trial, subscriptions and promotions. Check the features your actual NoGuru setup requires.

What the two subscriptions add up to

With Cornix Advanced on monthly billing, the first-100 NoGuru rate adds up to $98.90 USD/month ($49 + $49.90). For later new NoGuru subscribers, the total is $148.90 USD/month ($99 + $49.90). Trading fees, payment and network costs, and any applicable taxes are additional. These totals describe subscription costs, not what trading will earn.

With annual Cornix, the first-100 combination is equivalent to $81.99 a month, or $131.99 for later new NoGuru subscribers. You pay $395.88 to Cornix upfront and NoGuru separately. Existing credits and applicable Cornix promotions can change the bill.

Exchange costs

Buy and sell fees, spreads and slippage vary by venue, pair, order and account tier. They are excluded from the published gross signal-model record.

Payment costs

Cornix's billing guide states that crypto subscription invoices add 5%. Network costs and possible mobile taxes are separate. NoGuru crypto payments can also incur network costs.

Trading capital

The funds used for orders stay at your exchange. They are neither a subscription fee nor protected savings. Open holdings can lose value or remain tied up.

Existing offers & entitlements

Check what applies to you.

Founding 5: three additional NoGuru months

Already qualified for Founding 5? Your entitlement adds three free NoGuru months to the 14-day trial after qualifying for a paid Cornix plan. A Cornix demo alone does not qualify. Check that the extra time appears in your membership before paying; this section explains existing entitlements and does not promise a new place.

Exchange and customer referral rewards

Existing referral terms describe one NoGuru month for a qualifying exchange account opened through its link, and one month for a referred subscriber after they have paid for three months. These access credits can stack. The exchange, country, referral link and credit shown in your membership must meet the offer conditions. Cornix charges remain separate.

Longer NoGuru subscriptions

Longer NoGuru subscription terms have separate upfront prices and payment periods. Read the full amount, covered period and applicable terms in your membership checkout before paying. Existing earned access remains separate.

Cornix promotions, including MEXC

Cornix currently advertises Premium features at no extra charge for connected MEXC accounts from 1 June 2026, with no fixed campaign end date. That does not confirm your country eligibility, NoGuru pairs, configured bot or any NoGuru reward. Do not choose a restricted exchange to obtain a promotion.

Read Cornix's MEXC campaign conditions ↗

A linked annual Cornix offer has its own eligibility conditions and excludes UK residents. Check Cornix service eligibility as well as the offer terms; a promotion does not override country restrictions.

Renewal, cancellation and refunds

The existing NoGuru terms describe manual crypto renewals and access ending without payment. They state that payments are non-refundable; applicable legal rights still need to be reflected in the final contract. Cornix has separate billing rules. Read the provider and terms status before making a commitment.
