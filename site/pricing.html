Pricing / checked 31 Oct 2026

Two services.
One clear cost picture.

NoGuru provides the strategy and signals. Cornix handles execution. Your exchange charges for trades, and your trading capital is separate from all subscription costs.

Educational information, not financial advice. Costs describe charges, not expected earnings or a recommended investment amount.

01 / NoGuru

$49 USD / month

Published founding rate

For eligible first-100 subscribers, retained while the subscription stays active. Remaining availability has not been verified. A lapsed subscription does not carry an unconditional lifetime price.

The published price for later new subscriptions is $99/month. Confirm your actual offer before paying.

02 / Cornix

$49.90 USD / month

Advanced, on monthly billing

Ordinary published monthly price, with three active Signals Bots. The annual option is $395.88 paid upfront: a $32.99 monthly equivalent, not a monthly bill.

Cornix has its own service eligibility rules, trial, subscriptions and promotions. Check the features your actual NoGuru setup requires.

Compare current Cornix plans ↗

The ordinary monthly example: $98.90

$49 NoGuru + $49.90 Cornix Advanced = $98.90/month, before trading, payment, network or applicable tax charges. This assumes founding eligibility and ordinary monthly Cornix billing. It is cost arithmetic, not a breakeven target or a suggested investment amount.

With annual Cornix, the equivalent is $81.99/month, but you pay $395.88 to Cornix upfront and NoGuru separately. Promotions and earned free periods can change these figures.

costs visible.

Small percentages still matter when trades repeat or gross gains are small.

Exchange costs

Buy and sell fees, spreads and slippage vary by venue, pair, order and account tier. They are excluded from the published gross signal-model record.

Payment costs

Cornix's billing guide states that crypto subscription invoices add 5%. Network costs and possible mobile taxes are separate. NoGuru crypto payments can also incur network costs.

Trading capital

The funds used for orders stay at your exchange. They are neither a subscription fee nor protected savings. Open holdings can lose value or remain tied up.

Existing offers & entitlements

Check what applies to you.

Founding 5: three additional NoGuru months

The published offer adds three free NoGuru months to the 14-day trial after qualifying for a paid Cornix plan; a Cornix demo does not qualify. Capacity and actual crediting are unverified. Existing earned entitlements must be honoured; this preview does not grant new places.

Exchange and customer referral rewards

The current site describes one NoGuru month for a qualifying exchange account opened through its link, and one month for a referred subscriber after they have paid for three months. It says these can stack. Verify eligible venue, jurisdiction, attribution and crediting before relying on a reward. These are published access credits, not a cash payment.

Longer NoGuru subscriptions

The published 3-, 6- and 12-month terms advertise 10%, 15% and 20% discounts respectively. Confirm the full amount, payment timing and applicable terms in the official membership flow.

Cornix promotions, including MEXC

Cornix currently advertises Premium features at no extra charge for connected MEXC accounts from 1 June 2026, with no fixed campaign end date. That does not confirm your country eligibility, NoGuru pairs, configured bot or any NoGuru reward. Do not choose a restricted exchange to obtain a promotion.

NoGuru also advertises a linked annual Cornix discount; the linked offer excludes UK residents. Checkout eligibility and discount application were not tested.

Renewal, cancellation and refunds

Current NoGuru terms describe manual crypto renewals, access ending without payment, and non-refundable payments. Cornix has separate billing rules. Provider identity and final NoGuru terms remain incomplete in this review preview and must be verified before a customer launch; no new cancellation or refund promise is created here.
